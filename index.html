System requirements

mOS 1.0 runs on 64-bit Intel and AMD computers, including Intel Macs such as the MacBook Air (2017).

Part Minimum Recommended
Processor64-bit Intel or AMDIntel Core i5 or better
RAM4 GB8 GB
Storage25 GB64 GB or more
USB stick (live)8 GB16 GB or more, USB 3
GraphicsAnyIntel HD 5000 or newer, for liquid glass

Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later) aren't supported in mOS 1.0.

Get mOS 1.0

The download is coming soon.

Live USB

  1. Download the mOS ISO.
  2. Write it to a USB stick with balenaEtcher.
  3. Plug it in and restart. On a Mac, hold Option while it starts.
  4. Choose the mOS USB, then mOS 1.0 (Live).

VMware

  1. Download the mOS ISO.
  2. Create a new virtual machine and choose the ISO.
  3. Give it at least 4 GB of RAM and 2 cores.
  4. Start it and follow the setup.

Trying mOS from a USB stick doesn't change anything on your computer. Updates and files are only kept once mOS is installed.
