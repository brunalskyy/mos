mOS 1.0 Amber

A desktop that feels like yours.

mOS is a free operating system for laptops and PCs. Use it like Windows, with apps that look like a phone. Try it from a USB stick or in VMware without touching your computer.

Get mOS Take a look

The real mOS desktop, version 0.6.1.

A taskbar you already know

Start, your apps and the clock sit where you'd expect. One button shows Wi-Fi, sound and battery. Click it for the Control Centre: dark mode, Night Shift, Bluetooth, brightness and volume.

Click the clock for your notifications and the calendar.

Widgets that help

A flower clock, At a glance with the date and the weather, and a battery ring on laptops. They pick up your colours.

And there's more

mOS Store

Thousands of apps and games from Flathub, with screenshots, reviews and a progress bar that shows the speed and time left.

Settings

One place for everything: Wi-Fi, wallpaper, icon shape, taskbar, Night Shift, fonts, printers and more.

Your colours

Pick a colour or take one from your wallpaper. Buttons, icons and menus all follow, in light or dark.

Icon shapes

Circle, squircle or rounded square. Every app follows, even ones you add later.

Updates

Software update tells you what's new and installs it when you say so.

mOS Account

Sign in to back up your settings and app list, and get Insider builds early.

What you need

mOS runs on 64-bit Intel and AMD computers, including Intel Macs like the MacBook Air (2017).

On a USB stick

  1. Download mOS.
  2. Put it on a USB stick with balenaEtcher.
  3. Restart with the stick in. On a Mac, hold Option.
  4. Pick the USB, then mOS.

In VMware

  1. Download mOS.
  2. Make a new virtual machine and pick the file.
  3. Give it 4 GB of memory, 2 cores and 3D graphics.
  4. Start it and follow the setup.

Trying mOS from a USB stick doesn't change anything on your computer.
