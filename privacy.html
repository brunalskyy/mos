Privacy notice

Last updated 21 November 2026

mOS is a hobby operating system made by Bruno Woznicki. This page explains what happens when you make an mOS Account.

What we store

  • Your email address
  • Your name, if you type one in
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google name and profile picture
  • When your account was made and when you last signed in

Your password is never stored in a way anyone can read. It's handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

Why

Only so you can sign in to mOS and the mOS website, reset your password, and (later) keep your mOS settings the same on every computer. We don't sell your data, show adverts or send marketing emails.

Who handles it

Accounts are run on Google Firebase. Google processes the data for us and may store it outside the UK. See Firebase's privacy information.

Deleting your account

Want your account gone? Open an issue at github.com/brunalskyy/mos/issues saying "delete my mOS Account" (don't post your email publicly; we'll get in touch). We delete the account and everything stored with it.

Children

If you're under 13, ask a parent or carer before making an mOS Account.
